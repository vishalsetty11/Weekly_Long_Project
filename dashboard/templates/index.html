🏗️ Medallion Pipeline Stages

RAW

Daily Bhavcopy files are fetched from the NSE website and stored dynamically inside MotherDuck.

BRONZE

Immutable data ingestion layer. Raw data is scrubbed using strict regex filters to remove ETFs, mutual funds, and non-equity indices.

SILVER

Data transformation layer. Calculates 50-week EMA, Wilder RSI, and filters for delivery percentages over 50%.

GOLD

High-conviction analytical snapshot layer. Merges price breakouts and 3X volume surges into historical views.

📊 Metric Columns Explained

Symbol Click opens the internal Fundamentals & SCD Audit page. Chart icon routes to TradingView.
Close Price Weekly close price. Green if above 50-week MA, red if below. Routes directly to Screener.in profile.
RSI (14) Computed using Wilder's Smoothing over a 14-period lookback. Filtered for momentum > 50.
50-week MA A true Exponential Moving Average (EMA) capturing structural long-term trend direction.
180D High Peak weekly close reached during the trailing 26-week window (~6 months) confirming price breakout.
3X Vol Mult Current weekly volume relative to its 180-day average. Signifies institutional accumulation.
